* notes on PCB
** all dip switches should be in "OFF"
** SDDET /TH2 should be in TH2 position, otherwise right (red) button doesn't work
** gpio 34-39 are input only, set as pwm out can cause 'panic', need to erase flash and re-load good yaml 
** I2So can not do pwm in fluidnc
** TB(white)=main switch, TH1(black)=SW-L, TH2(purple)=SW-R/Cancel, needs jumper change
** J1-MPG, most MPGs work at 3.3V, but some may need 5v, which needs PCB solder change
** available gpio: X/Y/Z limits, MT_DET, 3DTouch, I2So: FAN1, FAN2, BEEPER, HE1, HE0, HBED
** sorted out BTN_EN1 (R2) and LCD_CLK (R3) to gpio-14 connection
   BTN_EN1 is on EXP2, and connected to MPG-EN1, seems BTN_EN1 is connected to esp32
   LCD_CLK is on EXP2, is connected to N_SCK to motor drivers spi bus
   Answer: LCD_CLK is not connected to gpio.14, but is connected to SPI_CLK with 0-ohm resistor
