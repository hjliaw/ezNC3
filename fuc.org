* frequently used commands
* esptool format flash with littlefs (required after fresh start)
  littlefs supports g-code in sub-directory
** esptool.py --chip esp32 --port /dev/ttyUSB0 write_flash 0x610000  ./release/littlefs2M.bin
   or 
   python /home/hjack/esp32/tools/esptool/esptool.py --chip esp32 ---
   4M starts at 0x38000  (512k)
   8M now starts at 0x61000  (2M space)
   
*** [not required]  write_flash -z --flash_mode dio --flash_freq 40m --flash_size detect
** esptool.py --- erase_flash
** esptool.py --- flash_id
** esptool.py --- read_flash 0x380000 0x80000 littlefs512k.bin
* fluidnc commands
** $localFS/format
** $localFS/list
** $localFS/run=file.gc
** $localfs/format=littlefs
** $localfs/migrate
   or 
   $localfs/backup          - copies everything on the local fs to /sd/localfs/*
   $localfs/format=littlefs - reformats the local fs to LittleFS
   $localfs/restore         - copies /sd/localfs/* to the local fs
* fluidterm ctrl-U to upload a file (slow)
  tested in new fluidnc at ~cnc/downloaded/FluidNC/.
